Players can choose whether to enable optional gameplay analytics and applicable advertising purposes. Refusing optional processing does not prevent access to core gameplay. 5. Sharing and security

Information may be transmitted to Google and Unity to provide the services described above. We do not directly sell players' personal information. We seek to minimize unnecessary collection, but no digital transmission or storage system is completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Third-party providers retain information according to their applicable policies and settings. Privacy preferences may be stored locally on the device. To request access, information about retention, or deletion where applicable, email devigrs96@gmail.com. 7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, and to withdraw consent. You may contact us using the email above to exercise applicable rights.

8. Children

We do not intentionally request personal information directly from children. Age-related advertising and data protection requirements apply according to the player's location and applicable rules. Parents or guardians may contact us about privacy concerns.
